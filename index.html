How to Use Photobooth

  1. Hold up both hands, thumb and index fingertip on each sketching a rectangle — a glowing frame appears live, matching where you hold your hands.
  2. Pinch your LEFT thumb and index together (one quick pinch) to cycle styles: Vintage B&W, Sepia, Vibrant Pop, Star Scrapbook. The current one shows at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Pinch your RIGHT thumb and index together and hold for a second to lock the frame and start a 4-second countdown. A photo captures automatically at zero, in whatever style is active.
  4. Each photo fills the strip on the left. Click the small save icon on any photo to download just that one at a custom size.
  5. After 4 photos, choose how to save the round: the combined strip, all 4 individually (square, or a size you draw with your hands), or both.
